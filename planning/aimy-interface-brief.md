# AImy Interface — Design Research Brief

**Active project:** AImy Interface

## Objective

Determine the detail language that makes an AI workspace feel dependable and effortless, and specify AImy's concretely.

## Research question (verbatim)

"What detail language — spacing scale, type ramp, colour and accent rules, state and motion conventions, presence and status signalling, and the format of action receipts — makes an AI workspace feel dependable and effortless, and what should AImy's be, concretely? Answer from published design-system documentation and reported practice of the Muse AI app, Claude, ChatGPT, Raycast, Arc, and Apple and Material guidelines, with named sources, and flag anything that is community lore rather than vendor-published."

## Paper title

A Detail Language for AImy

## Required structure

- Principles tied to evidence
- Buildable token spec (spacing, type, colour, state, motion)
- Anti-patterns in the current AImy UI
- Recommendations on three open decisions:
  1. Chat as home vs a dashboard containing chat
  2. Calm default with a Studio expansion vs studio by default
  3. Projects as a top-level destination vs inside the workspace panel

## Execution

- Research depth: deep, output: paper
- Spawned as background job (long-running)
- Deliverable presented in the drawer on completion
